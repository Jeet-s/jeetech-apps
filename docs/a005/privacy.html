Privacy Policy

TOEFL Preparation App (com.jeetech.a005) · JeeTech · last updated 2026-10-30

This policy explains what information TOEFL Preparation App (“the app”) and the services it uses collect, and how it is used.

Your data

The app works without an account and stores what you enter (for example your items, entries, notes and settings) on your device. To back it up, the app creates an anonymous account and saves a copy of this data on our cloud servers (Supabase, encrypted in transit and at rest). If you choose to sign in, we also store your email address, used only to send sign-in codes and to keep your data across devices — never for marketing. We do not collect your contacts, photos or location.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and all its cloud data at any time in the app (Settings → Account → Delete account) or on the web at https://app-cloud-api-production.up.railway.app/cloud/delete-account?app=com.jeetech.a005. Deletion is immediate and permanent; backups of the database are overwritten within 30 days.

AI features

When you use an AI feature (Speech-to-text transcript of Listen & Repeat and Interview recordings (needs audio-input support in the proxy); Rubric scoring on 0-30 and 1-6 scales across fluency, intelligibility, repeat accuracy, language use and organization, labelled Estimated with a range; Strengths and weaknesses list (2+ each) with a fix-this-next drill suggestion; Interview relevance check against the question; Improved answer rewrite from the user's own transcript at the next band, with edits highlighted; Word-level repeat-accuracy diff against the target sentence; Mock test report with section score and study plan; Weak-spot drill selection from the construct profile), the text you submit for that feature is sent through our server to an AI provider (Anthropic) to produce the result, together with an anonymous account identifier used for daily usage limits and abuse prevention. It is not used to train AI models, is not sold, and is kept by the provider for at most 30 days for abuse monitoring. Don't submit sensitive personal information to AI features. AI output can be wrong — check anything important.

Speaking features: the app uses the microphone only while you answer, and your phone's built-in speech recognition turns your voice into text on your device. The audio is never uploaded or stored by us; only the text transcript is sent to the AI feature, as above.

Anonymous usage statistics

To improve the app's first-use experience, it sends anonymous usage events to our own server (for example “onboarding finished”, “main feature used”, “settings screen opened”) together with a random identifier created on your device at install. These events never contain anything you type or store in the app, and contain no name, email, advertising ID, device ID or location, so they cannot be used to identify you; they are kept for at most 90 days and are never sold or shared. Uninstalling the app deletes the random identifier.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version may show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process your device's advertising ID, IP address (used for approximate location), device and app information, and ad interaction data to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise them. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws we ask for your consent through Google's consent message before ads are requested; you can change your choice or reset your advertising ID in Android settings. Pro and “Remove Ads” turn ads off. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases (Google Play Billing)

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never receive your payment details; Google shares only a purchase confirmation so the purchase can be unlocked and restored. Google's handling is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Diagnostics

Google Play may provide us with anonymous crash and performance reports (Android vitals) that do not identify you.

Notifications (Firebase Cloud Messaging)

If you allow notifications, the app registers a push token from Google Firebase Cloud Messaging with our server, together with a random install identifier, so we can send reminders and updates. No name, email or advertising ID is attached. You can turn notifications off at any time in your device settings; uninstalling the app removes the token.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Data is shared only with Google as described above, and is encrypted in transit.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices and deletion

You can delete all data stored by the app at any time in Settings → Delete all data, or by uninstalling the app. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings. For any privacy request, contact us at the address below.

Changes

We will update this page if our practices change; the date above shows the latest revision.
